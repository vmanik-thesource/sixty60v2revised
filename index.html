Why trust us

Built from what shoppers told us

We interviewed people who order groceries online every week, and changed the design based on what they said.

shoppers had recent orders that could have arrived later the same day.

3 of 3

add items to reach a minimum basket, so free Anytime starts at one clear amount: R450 linked, or R350 for members.

3 of 3

would pay a small fee for speed only when it's urgent, so Express stays and planned shops get cheaper.

Your money and your data

Money Market accounts and the wallet run with Money Market's registered banking partner. Every link is confirmed with a one-time PIN.

We use your order history to suggest windows only if you opt in, and you can switch it off at any time. Your data is handled under POPIA and is never sold.

What if I'm not home in my window?

We message you when the driver is 15 minutes away. You can name someone to receive the order.

Can I change my window?

Yes, until picking starts, in the app.

What if I need something urgently?

Choose Express 60 on that order. Flex never removes it.

Do I have to pay from the wallet?

No. The R13.50 Anytime fee, and free Anytime over R450, come with a linked Money Market account. Pay by card or wallet.

How do I top up my wallet?

With cash at any till, by EFT, or from your Money Market account.

Will my chilled items stay cold?

Chilled and frozen items are packed cold for your window. If something arrives spoiled, report it within 24 hours for a refund.
